Extension · Productivity

AI → PDF

Problem
Saving an AI conversation as PDF drags along menus, buttons and sidebars.
Idea
A clean view of the conversation, ready to print or save.
Plan
Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and other AIs; goes through the whole chat and keeps code, tables and formulas.
Done
Published on the Chrome Web Store. Free, no data collection.

Extension · Social media

Dezombify

Problem
Social networks fill your home feed with “For you”, suggestions, viral videos and ads, burying what the people you follow post.
Idea
Kill the algorithm: see only what the accounts you chose to follow post.
Plan
Always opens the feed of people you follow and hides recommendations, trends and promoted posts on X, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Threads, Bluesky, Twitch and LinkedIn. Per-network options: reposts, Reels, Shorts…
Done
Published on the Chrome Web Store. Free, no data collection.
